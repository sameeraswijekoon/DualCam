DualCam for Zoom - Web Version v2

Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox and file:// pages can block cameras. Best is the hosted HTTPS link version. Steps: 1) Plug in both cameras, close Zoom / Teams (they lock cameras). 2) Click Detect Cameras and Allow. 3) Check both small previews show a picture. 4) Layout = Side by Side, click START. 5) In Zoom: Share Screen > this browser window.

Clean Output hides all buttons and previews - only the combined video stays. Use it before Zoom Share Screen, or in OBS Window Capture. Press Esc or click the video to bring controls back.

Status: click Detect Cameras first.
